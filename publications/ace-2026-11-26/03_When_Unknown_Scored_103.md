# When Unknown Scored 103

*A missing number became an excellent opportunity. The arithmetic was doing exactly what it was told.*

**Rajesh Singh · The NeuroAlchemist | MetaForgeX AI**

**Editorial status:** Author-approved essay, 26 November 2026. Original exported-function behaviour reproduced on synthetic inputs; subsequent repairs reported by Claude, not independently verified here.

A missing answer received an excellent score.

We were reviewing the calculation export from my Claude-built Rentier Monitor, a project that follows ownership, dependency and the changing economics of AI. The review used synthetic inputs. It found that an unknown time-to-cash received the fastest rating. When capital was also unknown, the opportunity score reached 103 on a nominal 100-point scale.

There was no need for an invented market forecast. The programming language had already supplied the crucial fiction: zero.

## The fact that entered through conversion

In JavaScript, Number(null) returns zero. That is specified behaviour. Whether it is appropriate for a business calculation depends on what null means in the input contract.

For a missing observation, zero can be an especially persuasive substitute. No waiting sounds attractive. No required capital sounds even better. A formula can reward both without ever asking whether either was measured.

The same exported functions turned a null labour index into a numeric share of zero and a null cash field into zero cash. A missing household-action trigger became a threshold of zero and received a definitive Later status at the supplied clock reading.

These were targeted function probes. They established possible behaviours in the exported implementation, not the prevalence of bad live decisions. Keeping that boundary clear is part of the finding.

## Reproduction does not settle meaning

The monitor's concentration ladder offered a related lesson. Its formula reproduced correctly for all 27 scored rows. The direction was also right: greater concentration produced a higher score.

One row nevertheless treated 8,512 registered persons as if they were equal-sized independent suppliers. Its score of zero followed the assumption perfectly. The registration count could not establish the assumed market structure.

Another row counted cloud regions. A region count can describe geographic presence. It does not tell us how much capacity each provider offers, or whether two services are substitutes for the workload a buyer needs.

These are different failures. One turns missing data into an apparently observed number. The other gives a valid observation a meaning its measurement does not support. Both can survive an arithmetic check.

## The correction has its own evidence status

Claude's return note reports that the affected functions and panels were changed after my approval. It also reports scanning the current tables: no affected rows in the opportunity, labour or household inputs, and one missing finance-lease value in the financial table. The existing exposure presentation had already described that case as a lower bound.

The repair reportedly preserves UNKNOWN, leaves invalid opportunities unranked and adds explicit missing-data indications. It has not been independently checked here against the revised code, and the note says browser visuals were not inspected.

A report of a repair belongs in the record. A reproduced repair requires another step. Treating the two as equivalent would repeat the underlying mistake in a more respectable form.

## What we should preserve before accelerating

The implications extend into ACE. Before moving a scoring workload through a faster numerical or compiled path, we need to know which distinctions must survive. Confirmed zero, unknown and not applicable should not collapse merely because a backend prefers a numeric array.

A validity mask or an explicit decision state may be mundane engineering. It can carry the most consequential information in the system.

The next bounded task is to replay the original probes against the corrected functions, preserve valid controls and inspect how the panels render missingness. Stale evidence and conflicting sources need their own controls later. This review does not establish a general missing-data framework, financial harm or a new commercial product.

It does establish a useful question to ask of every attractive score: which observations earned its inputs?

The score can wait until the evidence arrives.
