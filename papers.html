Working Paper 2026-02 | Revision 2.0

The Entropy of Choice

A Boundary-Complexity Hypothesis for Decision Intelligence

Proposes that useful policy capacity may depend more on the complexity of action-changing regions than on the full volume of the state space. The paper presents a covering-number argument and a falsifiable experimental programme.

Independent working paper. Not peer-reviewed. Cross-field correspondences remain subject to formal and empirical validation.

Mathematical Supplement 2026-02A | Revision 2.0

The Entropy of Choice: Mathematical Appendices

Mathematical Appendices to the Boundary-Complexity Hypothesis

States the boundary covering bound under explicit assumptions, narrows the rate-distortion and free-energy claims, separates the physical analogy from the mathematical result, and defines a concrete falsification protocol.

Independent mathematical supplement. Not peer-reviewed. Quantum and hardware implications are conditional research questions.

What changed in Revision 2.0

General dimension

The boundary expression now uses the intrinsic dimension k. The epsilon-squared form appears only as a special case.

Calibrated correspondences

Rate-distortion, variational free energy and holographic entropy are no longer described as universally identical results.

Falsification first

The papers now specify synthetic recovery, boundary-shell localisation, representation stability and capacity-knee tests.
