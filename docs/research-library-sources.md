# Research library additions — 8 October 2026

External references inform the programme; they do not validate NSDM or ACE. Metadata and author abstracts/source summaries were checked. This is a selected reading path, not a systematic literature review, full-text review or independent replication. Publisher access may vary; sources are linked, not rehosted.

## Neural-Symbolic Computing: An Effective Methodology for Principled Integration of Machine Learning and Reasoning

Garcez et al. (2019) · Survey / arXiv manuscript

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.06088

Relevance: Connect learned representations to explicit reasoning.

Limit: Integration is a design approach; it does not by itself validate an NSDM decision.

## On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks

Guo et al. (2017) · ICML research paper

Source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html

Relevance: Separate confidence scores from observed correctness.

Limit: Calibration on one evaluation distribution does not guarantee calibration after deployment shift.

## A Gentle Introduction to Conformal Prediction and Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification

Angelopoulos & Bates (2021; revised 2022) · Tutorial / arXiv manuscript

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511

Relevance: Study prediction sets, coverage and the cost of uncertainty.

Limit: Standard coverage guarantees require assumptions such as exchangeability; marginal coverage is not individual certainty.

## Roofline: An Insightful Visual Performance Model for Floating-Point Programs and Multicore Architectures

Williams, Waterman & Patterson (2008) · Berkeley technical report

Source: https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/2008/EECS-2008-134.html

Relevance: Relate operational intensity, data movement and performance bounds.

Limit: A performance model is not a measurement of our hardware or proof of decision correctness.

## Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention

Kwon et al. (2023) · SOSP paper / arXiv version

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180

Relevance: Understand dynamic KV-cache storage and serving efficiency.

Limit: Published speedups belong to the authors’ configurations; they are not local ACE results.

## Concrete Problems in AI Safety

Amodei et al. (2016) · Research agenda / arXiv manuscript

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565

Relevance: Turn objectives, side effects and distribution shift into testable failure questions.

Limit: A problem taxonomy is not evidence that safety or alignment has been solved.

## Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases

Tversky & Kahneman (1974) · Science research article

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17835457/

Relevance: Examine representativeness, availability and anchoring.

Limit: Classic findings require contextual testing; they do not diagnose an individual user.

## Neuromarketing: the hope and hype of neuroimaging in business

Ariely & Berns (2010) · Nature Reviews Neuroscience review

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2875927/

Relevance: Separate useful measurement questions from claims about reading consumers’ minds.

Limit: Neural signals and behavioural proxies need construct validity, consent and independent evaluation.
